Ferret trachea tubes (VH Lab)
=============================

Source: ferret_trachea_tube.scad (OpenSCAD, parametric). All dimensions in mm.
Regenerate an STL with, e.g.:
  openscad -D variant=2 -o trachea_tube_4mm_tip_30mm.stl ferret_trachea_tube.scad

Measured from the insertion tip (z = 0):
  0-5      tip section, 45 degree bevel, OD = tip OD
  5-10     taper from tip OD to body OD (to rib OD on ribbed tubes, flush with the first rib)
  10-(L-6) body; on ribbed tubes, alternating 1 mm ribs (body OD + 1) and 1 mm grooves (body OD)
           each rib's tip-facing side slopes up over 0.6 mm (for insertion); back side is square (holds the tie)
  (L-6)-(L-5)  1 mm neck, 4 mm OD (gives the barb a shoulder)
  (L-5)-L  respirator connector barb: 5 mm OD shoulder tapering to 4 mm OD, last 2 mm straight 4 mm OD
  Wall thickness 0.5 mm throughout; connector ID 3 mm.
  Suture tab (all tubes): rises 3 mm above the tube surface (rib tops on ribbed
  tubes), 6 mm long, 1.5 mm thick, with a 2 mm transverse hole for suture thread.
  The hole's bottom edge is flush with the tube surface so the suture pulls
  toward the animal with no lever arm (no rotational torque on the tube).
  Centred 20 mm from the tip on the 30 mm tubes and 15 mm from the tip on the
  25 mm tube (same 10 mm distance from the connector end, clear of the barb).
