Ferret trachea tubes (VH Lab)
=============================

Source: ferret_trachea_tube.scad (OpenSCAD, parametric). All dimensions in mm.
Regenerate an STL with, e.g.:
  openscad -D variant=2 -o trachea_tube_4mm_tip_30mm.stl ferret_trachea_tube.scad

Measured from the insertion tip (z = 0):
  0-5      tip section, 45 degree bevel, OD = tip OD
  5-10     taper from tip OD to body OD
  10-(L-6) body; on ribbed tubes, alternating 1 mm ribs (body OD + 1) and 1 mm grooves (body OD)
  (L-6)-(L-5)  1 mm neck, 4 mm OD (gives the barb a shoulder)
  (L-5)-L  respirator connector barb: 5 mm OD shoulder tapering to 4 mm OD, last 2 mm straight 4 mm OD
  Wall thickness 0.5 mm throughout; connector ID 3 mm.
  Suture tab (ribbed tubes): centred 20 mm from tip, rises 5 mm above rib OD,
  6 mm long, 1.5 mm thick, 2 mm transverse hole for suture thread.
